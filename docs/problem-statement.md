# CivicLens-AI

## Problem Statement

AI Triage for Citizen Complaints.

Citizens report potholes, overflowing garbage,
broken streetlights, water leaks and fallen trees.

Our system converts unstructured complaints into
structured and prioritised municipal tickets.

## Main Objectives

1. Accept complaints in English and regional languages.
2. Classify complaints into categories.
3. Assign severity levels.
4. Route complaints to the correct department.
5. Display priority-based tickets on a dashboard.
